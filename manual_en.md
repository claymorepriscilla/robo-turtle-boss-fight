## 📜 1. The Mission Story

Deep inside Sector-7, the ancient silicon forest has been overrun by the giant cybernetic dreadnought **ROBO-TURTLE**. It drains the ecosystem's power grid to feed its robotic swarm of sub-bugs and molten fire cores. Armed with a **High-Density Energy Laser**, you must infiltrate the arena, bypass its devastating physical shockwaves, and target its glowing reactor eye to save the forest.

## 🖥️ 2. The Arcade HUD (Heads-Up Display)

When inside the arcade cabinet, keep a close watch on your display metrics:

*   **SCORE Indicator (Top Center)**: Displays your current score (starts at `1250`).
*   **HI-SCORE Record (Top Right)**: Persists your all-time highest record using local memory storage.
*   **Player Health (Top Left)**: Represented by glowing retro pixel hearts (starts with `3` out of `4` maximum heart containers).
*   **Boss Health Bar (Center Top)**: Displays the Robo-Turtle’s remaining health percentage (`0% - 100%`). The bar flashes white when the boss is taking damage.

## 🕹️ 3. Complete Controls Guide

Whether you are playing on a desktop keyboard or a mobile touchscreen, the controls are responsive and fine-tuned for high-speed action.

### ⌨️ Keyboard Layout (Desktop)

| Action | Keys | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Move Left** | `A` or `◀ Left Arrow` | Walks left. Face direction shifts left. |
| **Move Right** | `D` or `▶ Right Arrow` | Walks right. Face direction shifts right. |
| **Jump** | `Spacebar`, `W`, or `▲ Up Arrow` | Perform a dynamic jump. Bounces higher with gravity. |
| **Fire Blaster** | `J`, `F`, or `Z` | Fires a rapid horizontal energy laser beam. |
| **Select / Fast Start** | `Enter` | Launches the game instantly from the Start screen. |
| **Restart Fight** | `R` | Instantly resets the game when in Game Over or Victory screens. |

### 📱 Touch Layout (Mobile & Tablet)

On mobile devices or screens under `1024px`, an on-screen arcade controller will automatically pop up. 
*   *You can also show or hide this control panel at any time by clicking the **Touch Controls: ON/OFF (🎮)** button on the cabinet controls panel.*

*   **◀ Button (Left)**: Hold to run left.
*   **▶ Button (Right)**: Hold to run right.
*   **🔥 SHOOT Button (Yellow)**: Tap to fire your energy laser beam.
*   **🦘 JUMP Button (Red)**: Tap to jump.
*   **Overlay Actions**: The **START MISSION**, **RETRY FIGHT**, and **PLAY AGAIN** buttons in the overlay screens are fully optimized for direct touch inputs!

## 🐢 5. Boss AI & Combat Phases

The giant cybernetic Robo-Turtle has `120 HP` and operates using a health-based phase transition and a random attack generator:

### 💥 Boss Attack Patterns

1.  **Flame Stream (`windup` $\rightarrow$ `shoot`)**: 
    The boss winds up (jaws flash red/orange) and spits a stream of heavy fireballs:
    *   *Standard Fireballs*: Travel straight with light tracking targeting your vertical coordinate.
    *   *Sine-Wave Fireballs*: Travel in a high-amplitude sinusoidal wave pattern. Harder to predict and time!
2.  **Earthquake Stomp (`slam`)**: 
    The boss rears back and slams its mechanical foot down. This shakes the camera heavily and fires **4 high-speed horizontal shockwaves** traveling along the platforms.
3.  **Swarm Summon (`roar`)**:
    The boss flashes neon-green and roars, spawning green flying bugs that patrol the sky and swoop down as aerial hazards.

### 🛡️ Boss Phases

*   **Phase 1 (100% - 50% HP)**: Standard attack velocities and delays.
*   **Phase 2 (Below 50% HP)**: 
    *   The reactor eye starts pulsing dynamically.
    *   All attacks are **speeded up by 40%**.
    *   Flame fireballs move with massive velocity.
    *   Stomp shockwaves travel much faster.
    *   Bug spawns are doubled!

## 💡 6. Combat Tips & Strategies

To master the battle and claim the number one spot on the leaderboards, employ these advanced techniques:

*   **Reactor Core Critical Hits**: 
    Aim for the Robo-Turtle’s front face (the glowing reactor eye). Hitting this region scores a **Critical Hit (Critical Yellow)**, dealing **5 damage** instead of the standard **3 damage**. This shortens the fight significantly!
*   **Weapon Recoil Jump**:
    Firing your laser blaster pushes you backwards slightly due to recoil. You can use this horizontal impulse to extend your jump length or keep yourself suspended over the hazard pit for extra fractions of a second!
*   **Mushroom Management**:
    Do not grab the Red Mushroom immediately if you are at 3 hearts and the boss is about to stomp. Wait for a safe opening. If you can dodge attacks and reach full health (4 hearts) before collecting it, you will secure the **+500 Max Health Bonus points**!
*   **Pipe Shelter**:
    The giant green pipe on the right (`x: 680-745`) serves as an excellent high-ground platform. Stand on top of it to shoot directly at the boss's eye level, or hide behind it to block incoming low-altitude shockwaves!
